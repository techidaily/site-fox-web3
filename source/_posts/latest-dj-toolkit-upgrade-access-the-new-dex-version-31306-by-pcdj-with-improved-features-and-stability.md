![DEX 3 with Party Tyme Karaoke](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/IMG_4893.jpg?fit=300%2C286&ssl=1 "DEX 3 With Party Tyme")

DEX 3.13.0.6 is a maintenance release that addresses a few issues and improves overall performance and stability.

Due to a recent change in file format protocols at [Shadertoy.com](https://www.shadertoy.com/), the Shader creation platform where you can collect and add Shader’s to DEX 3 for real-time, beat aware visualizations, shaders were not installing properly to DEX 3\. The 3.13.0.6 update addresses the issue and you can once again install additional shaders for DEX 3 from [Shadertoy.com](https://www.shadertoy.com/). You can also find shaders that we gathered for our customers on these two previous blog posts: [Shader Roundup 1](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) [Shader Roundup 2](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

The 3.13.0.6 update of our DEX 3 DJ software also includes improved overall support for both built-in subscription services, [SoundCloud](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [Party Tyme Karaoke](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), as well as the PartyTyme.net karaoke store.

A number of other small bug fixes and performance optimizations are included.
